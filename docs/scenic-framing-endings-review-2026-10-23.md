## Durable builder correction

The failed mirror attempts exposed a production authoring defect. The
builder generated reflection requirements from input terrain, then validated
a rebaked result against that stale requirement set. A bake can activate a
new mirror or move its sampled water plane. Rebaked coverage failures were
also suppressed by a budget path, allowing fitting to return on resource
success before truthful final coverage admission.

The correction derives tour and retained-rail mirror requirements from the
**final decoded terrain**, validates both budgets, and refuses fitting before
packing when coverage fails. An optional independent `railPoses` baker
channel expresses ordinary rail limits separately from tour-scaled poses.
Absent/empty channel cases retain prior manifest and compressed/decoded
payload bytes in four direct compatibility checks.

Independent review found a P2 in the rail-only channel's occlusion clipping.
The correction uses the clipped arguments when either explicit pose channel
is present, with a real regression covering absent/empty tour poses. Local
review identity is `e07cd84d2fc28f32c5a814fa5f9e8a3e87006d0b`, following
`781c7d2ce01dab0942c09edfc10f90245e8dd9c4`. Scoped verification reports
**68/68 passing**, lint zero and preserved default/empty/mixed byte behavior.
This fixes authoring admission; it does not grant Black image acceptance.
