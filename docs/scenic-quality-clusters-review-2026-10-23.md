## Black: retain lighting and memory fixes, close forest iteration

The first quality prototype, its calibrated revision and the final projected-
crown experiment were all rejected from actual images against the original
R7 representation. The final experiment at
`f3d20fd4df5bac30c0f2173827d930d3594564e8` used an analytic fallback below
2 actual scenic pixels, restoring mesh through 4 pixels. Root and independent
review rejected the classic and detailed-grid results. The earlier provisional
84-second observation did not establish acceptance after the complete
comparison.

CPU diagnosis found both raster undersampling and intrinsic sparse branch
coverage. Passing assembly/projection tests did not establish a better forest
image. The rejected filter shared instance attributes but added six program
variants, 18 scene/depth objects, up to 234,098 submitted analytic triangles and
a modeled 524,288 B program allowance. Those mechanisms and costs are removed
from the retained renderer, along with the experimental near-mesh radius policy.
Original R7 forest placement and representation are restored.

The retained renderer contains only the original R7 forest representation,
Black's static **0.32 receiver fill floor** and the separate preparation-phase
and closure-lifetime fixes. Root inspected classic 16/49/64/84/128/156/end and
true detailed-grid 84/end controls. At **128 seconds**, the ridge and green
foreground are clearer; final layer separation improves modestly, and the
warm **156-second dusk** remains. Retain this bounded lighting improvement.
The forest still looks soft/stippled. That defect is documented and receives
no further rendering iteration in this workstream.

Both retained captures passed: **657 full-song frames and seven detailed-grid
controls**, with zero residency denials or overcommits. The observed aggregate
peaks are **487,665,092 B** full/classic and **490,890,692 B** detailed-grid,
